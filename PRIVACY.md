# Privacy Policy — AI CV Maker

_Last updated: 2026-05-17_

This privacy policy describes how the **AI CV Maker** mobile and web
application ("the App") handles your information.

## Information we store on your device

The App is **local-first**. All of the following data is stored only on
the device where you create it, in the App's private storage:

- The contents of your CVs / resumes (name, contact details, work
  experience, education, skills, projects, certifications, languages,
  references).
- Your preferences (selected country, chosen theme, default template).
- Your Google Gemini API key, if you choose to provide one. The key is
  stored in the device's secure keystore (Android Keystore / iOS
  Keychain). On web it is stored in browser local storage.

We do **not** operate a backend server. We do **not** upload your CVs to
any server. We do **not** create user accounts.

## Information sent to third parties

The App uses **Google's Gemini API** to power AI rewriting features, but
only when:

1. You provide your own Google Gemini API key in Settings, **and**
2. You explicitly tap the "Improve with AI" button on a field, or use
   the AI suggestion screen.

When that happens, the specific text you have entered into that field
(plus the country context — e.g. "UK", "US") is sent directly from your
device to Google's Gemini API for processing. The rewritten text is
returned and shown to you. Nothing else is sent.

We do not log, store, or proxy these requests through any of our servers
— they go directly from your device to Google.

Google's processing of these requests is governed by Google's API
Terms and Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you do not provide a Gemini API key, no AI requests are made and no
data leaves your device beyond what your operating system normally does.

## Information we do not collect

- We do not collect analytics or telemetry.
- We do not track you across apps or websites.
- We do not show advertising.
- We do not sell or share your data.

## Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.

## Your choices

- You can delete a CV at any time from the "My CVs" screen. Deleted CVs
  are removed from device storage immediately.
- You can remove your stored Gemini API key from the Settings screen.
- Uninstalling the App deletes all stored CVs and preferences.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support channel
listed on the App's store page.
